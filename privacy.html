Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

The short version

The tools on this website run inside your browser. What you type or paste into them stays on your device. We do not collect it, store it or send it to our servers.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not use cookies.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We do not save the text, tokens, passwords or files you use in the tools.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep technical logs, such as your IP address, when you visit a page. This is handled by GitHub under its own privacy statement, and we do not receive or use that information.

Things to be aware of

  • Markdown Previewer. If your Markdown contains an image from another website, your browser loads that image from that website, which can see your IP address, as it would on any web page.
  • Copy buttons. When you press a copy button, the text is placed in your device clipboard. We do not read your clipboard.
  • Links. Links to other websites are not controlled by us, and their privacy practices may differ.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. If we ever add analytics or advertising, we will describe it here before we do.

Contact

If you have a question about privacy, please open an issue on GitHub.
