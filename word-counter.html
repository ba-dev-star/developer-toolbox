How to use the Word and Character Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. The numbers update as you type: words, characters, characters without spaces, lines, paragraphs and reading time.
  3. Press Clear to start again.

About this tool

This counter shows how long your text is. It is useful for essays, articles, social media posts, product descriptions and any text with a length limit. Your text stays in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

How are words counted?

Words are groups of characters separated by spaces or new lines. This works for English and Arabic. Text without spaces, such as Chinese or Japanese, is counted as one word for each unbroken run of characters.

How is the reading time calculated?

It assumes an average reading speed of 200 words per minute, rounded up to the next minute.

What counts as a paragraph?

A block of text separated from the next one by an empty line.

Are emoji counted correctly?

Yes. Each emoji counts as one character.

Is my text saved?

No. Nothing is uploaded or stored.
