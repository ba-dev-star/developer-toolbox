About Developer Toolbox

A free collection of everyday tools for developers. Fast, simple and private.

What is it?

Developer Toolbox brings together the small tools that programmers use every day: formatting JSON, decoding a JWT, testing a regular expression, converting a timestamp, generating a hash and many more. Each tool is on its own page, loads quickly and does one job well.

Our principles

  • Free. Every tool is free to use, with no limits.
  • Private. The tools run in your browser. The text, tokens and files you paste are not sent to our servers.
  • No sign up. Open a tool and use it. There are no accounts.
  • Fast. The pages are small and do not load heavy libraries.

Browse the tools

You can see all the tools, search them and filter them by category on the home page.

Suggestions and problems

Found a bug, or want a tool that is not here? Please tell us by opening an issue on GitHub. Every suggestion is read.

A note about accuracy

We test the tools carefully, but they are provided as they are. Please double check important results, especially for security work. For example, the Hash Generator and the JWT Decoder do not replace server-side checks.
