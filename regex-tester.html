Quick reference

\d a digit
\w a letter, digit or _
\s a space or tab
. any character
^ $ start and end
+ * ? repeat
{2,4} 2 to 4 times
[abc] one of a, b, c
(...) capture group
(?<name>...) named group
a|b a or b
\b word boundary
Some very complex patterns can make the browser slow down or freeze. If that happens, simplify the pattern or close the tab.
